# D1B — Hirearchy Frontend Craft

## Status

Accepted D1 foundation: `33850c872c19128bc7493a0b58bc236ecfe10684`.

## Goal

Turn the accepted foundation into a highly crafted, memorable masterbrand
experience without changing product truth or engineering architecture.

## Brand

Hirearchy masterbrand. Current product: Hirearchy Software.

Working brand line: **Evidence over impressions.**

Personality: precise, calm, curious, respectful, assured. Expression: about 70%
serious and 30% expressive.

## Core visual principle

Progressive Clarity: **LESS ASSUMPTION → MORE CONTEXT**.

Never frame the story as wrong → right, bad → good, winner → loser, or
score → verdict.

## Visual grammar

- **Adaptive Frame:** recognizable spatial grammar that opens, divides,
  focuses, adapts, and creates context.
- **Signal Field:** sparse, meaningful evidence fragments whose relationships
  become clearer.
- **Evidence Narrative:** real-work events gain meaning as context unfolds.

## Motion grammar

Motion must organize, reframe, unfold, prioritize, adapt, enter, contrast,
settle, focus, or transition. It must perform a semantic action, not decorate.
Adjacent sections must not share the same dominant motion idea. Premium means
restraint, pacing, intention, and silence as well as movement.

## Homepage narrative

1. Hero — organize / relate
2. Impressions — reframe
3. Work leaves evidence — unfold
4. Evidence to clarity — prioritize / focus
5. Product family — adapt
6. Hirearchy Software — enter environment
7. Campaign — contrast
8. CTA — settle

## D1B ownership

**Antigravity:** Hero / Adaptive Frame refinement, Impressions, Campaign,
section transitions, typography choreography, micro-interactions, visual
polish.

**Codex:** frontend craft architecture, Evidence Narrative, Evidence to
Clarity, Product Family expression, Hirearchy Software bridge, responsive /
accessibility / performance hardening, testing, documentation, final
integration.

Ownership is primary responsibility, not permission to damage adjacent
sections.

## Anti-AI-template constraints

No generic startup cards, bento-for-everything, glassmorphism, neon,
purple/blue AI gradients, glowing nodes, decorative networks, telemetry/HUD
graphics, fake dashboards or terminals, decorative code, blobs, particles,
repeated fade-up/translateY choreography, AI-marketing vocabulary, or visual
complexity without semantic purpose.

## Copy and engineering boundaries

No unsupported hiring claims, automated judgment language, or candidate
scores/rankings as truth. The final hiring decision remains human. AI in the
candidate environment is tooling, not judge.

Do not alter backend/domain behavior, evidence semantics, session lifecycle,
auth/trust, timing authority, AI authority chain, or candidate/evaluator
epistemic boundaries. D1B is masterbrand/frontend craft.

## Quality contract

Each section must remain strong when motion is paused, use an intentional
composition and semantic motion purpose, stay responsive and accessible,
support reduced motion, and remain distinct from generic AI SaaS.

Preserve eight semantic sections, current brand language, human decision
authority, future-product honesty, motion order, real navigation destinations,
and the absence of candidate ranking/scoring claims.

## Foundation decisions

- Section markup lives in section-owned modules under `apps/web/app/home/`;
  `page.tsx` owns only header and composition. `AdaptiveFrame` and the shared
  arrow glyph remain the only shared homepage primitives.
- `home.css` stays centralized. Its selectors already expose section ownership,
  while tokens, breakpoints, motion, and reduced-motion rules benefit from one
  ordered cascade.
- The isolated Impressions `01 / 06` index is removed rather than expanded into
  a decorative numbering system.
- Product Family keeps its accepted static grid as fallback. Modern browsers,
  including Safari 16+, support track animation; older browsers may step to the
  final layout, which remains complete and readable. No script or duplicate
  transform animation is justified for a progressive enhancement.
