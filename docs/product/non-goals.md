# MVP non-goals

The MVP does not include:

- candidate scoring, ranking, competence percentages, or an “AI Judgment” model;
- ATS integrations, recruiter analytics, organization management, enterprise RBAC, or billing;
- multiple technology stacks, automatic scenario generation, or a scenario marketplace;
- webcam capture, biometrics, full-device monitoring, or complex anti-cheat systems;
- autonomous AI coding agents;
- recruiter dashboards, scorecards, recommendation engines, analytics warehouses, or candidate personality profiles;
- Kubernetes, microservices, or complex event-streaming infrastructure.

These are not implied roadmap commitments. Any future addition needs a real validated requirement and an explicit decision.
