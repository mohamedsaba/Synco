## 1. Visual Diagnosis of the Reference

Inspection of `hirearchy-website-direction-v1.png` reveals a conceptual 8-panel storyboard sheet created to explore an interactive journey rather than a conventional SaaS marketing grid.

### 1.1 Intentional Triumphs (What Works & Must Be Preserved)

1. **The STRATA Architectural Motif:**
   The three-layer ascending stepped mark (STRATA) provides a natural structural language. Layers do not just sit as an icon; in Panels 01, 04, and 05, they function as tilted spatial planes, organizational dividers, and perspective substrates that separate information by depth.
2. **Extreme Typographic Contrast:**
   The visual rhythm relies on massive, tightly tracked, high-density editorial display headlines (`"Real work. In context."`, `"Traditional hiring misses the real picture."`) counterweighted against micro-scale, hyper-precise functional metadata (`01`, `SESSION 0427`, timestamps `00:14:27`, `00:02:14`, file tags).
3. **Warm Daylight Canvas Intersected by Carbon Workbenches:**
   The primary background is an unbleached, architectural warm light tone (`#F4F1EA` / `#EDE8DD`), creating daylight openness. Product environments and evidence artifacts cut into this daylight as dark carbon/slate workbenches (`#141416`), providing dramatic contrast moments without making the entire website feel oppressive or gamer-dark.
4. **Physicality of Evidence:**
   Rather than flat abstract vector icons, evidence is rendered as tangible physical plates—translucent crystalline sheets, stacked cards, and depth-separated inspection panels.
5. **Interactive Presentation / Narrative Arc:**
   The reference is structured as an unfolding filmic journey: Problem → Mechanical Reality → Structured Context → Live Product Workbench → Multi-stakeholder Utility → Indelible Thesis.

### 1.2 Dangerous Traps & Flaws (What Must Be Systematically Rejected)

1. **Luxury / Crypto Color Grading:**
   The reference exhibits a bronze/gold/champagne metallic sheen and warm yellow-gold edge glows. This evokes private wealth management, luxury watches, or Web3 speculative tokens. Hirearchy is a rigorous, objective engineering evaluation substrate. The luxury sheen must be replaced with an industrial, intellectual, material palette.
2. **Violations of Product Truth (Prohibited Claims in Panels 04 & 07):**
   - Panel 07 contains unsupported speed, quality, and throughput claims. These are generic SaaS velocity tropes that violate Hirearchy Software product truth; Hirearchy does _not_ claim speed miracles or better hiring decisions. It provides observable evidence for human review.
   - Panel 04 implies automated scoring summaries (`"Session timeline"`, clean linear completion bars). In product truth, real engineering work is nonlinear: candidates explore, get stuck, backtrack, inspect docs, test hypotheses, and fail before succeeding.
3. **Floating UI Panels Without Mechanical Justification:**
   In Panels 01 and 05, UI cards float in arbitrary 3D space with soft multi-directional blur. In the final interactive build, depth must represent **epistemic stratification** (layers of chronological record), not decorative zero-gravity drift.
4. **Generic AI Glows and Glassmorphism:**
   Blurred backdrop filters and neon rim lights must be replaced with crisp, physical architectural translucency (mineral glass, tracing paper, mechanical overlays) and directional daylight casting physical ambient occlusion.

## 2. Core Creative Thesis

> **"The Architecture of Evidence: Stratigraphic Reconstruction of Human Work."**

In geology, _stratigraphy_ reveals the true history of the earth through physical, observable layers deposited over time. No algorithm is required to invent the past; the strata speak for themselves.

Hirearchy operates on the identical principle:

- **Resumes and short interviews are flat projections**—compressed, distorted summaries stripped of time, struggle, and context.
- **Hirearchy Software is a stratigraphic recording instrument**—it captures the chronological, observable reality of engineering problem-solving (attempts, investigations, revisions, verifications, outcomes, and uncertainties) and presents them as an uncompressed, structured evidentiary record.
- **The Evaluator owns the verdict**—the system provides legible depth; the human evaluator applies judgment.

The visual identity embodies this: **The STRATA three-layer ascending mark is the master spatial engine of the site.** Elements separate into three depth strata, slide along chronological axes, and snap into crystal-clear alignment when context is established.

## 3. Final Proposed Website Narrative

The experience is structured as an **8-scene continuous interactive presentation**, organized into 3 overarching dramatic acts with 3 distinct motion energy peaks:

- **ACT I: THE ARTIFACT OF TRUTH (Exposition & Fracture)**
  - **Scene 01: OPENING — Real Work. In Context.** _(MOTION PEAK 1)_
    _The thesis stated. The spatial depth of real engineering work revealed._
  - **Scene 02: THE LIMITATION — The Flat Projection.** _(Quiet Valley)_
    _Why traditional signals fail: Resumes, short quizzes, and leetcode puzzles compress away the dimensions that matter._
  - **Scene 03: OBSERVABLE WORK — The Stratigraphy of an Attempt.** _(Building Rhythm)_
    _Work is not a static score. It is an observable sequence of actions._

- **ACT II: THE INSTRUMENT (Revelation & Synthesis)**
  - **Scene 04: STRATIFIED CONTEXT — Sequence Creates Legibility.** _(Controlled Rise)_
    _How isolated events lock into chronological strata._
  - **Scene 05: PRODUCT REVEAL — The Inhabited Workbench.** _(MOTION PEAK 2)_
    _The core product emerges through a scripted, tactile multi-pane representation of Hirearchy Software._
  - **Scene 06: MULTI-PERSPECTIVE RECONSTRUCTION — Three Lenses, One Reality.** _(Calm Functional Focus)_
    _Engineering Lead, Recruiter, and Hiring Manager inspect the identical evidentiary record from distinct operational altitudes._

- **ACT III: THE MONUMENT (Climax & Resolution)**
  - **Scene 07: CAMPAIGN CLIMAX — Evidence Over Impressions.** _(MOTION PEAK 3)_
    _The monolithic manifesto: Typographic monumentalism meets tectonic strata._
  - **Scene 08: CLOSE & COLOPHON — The Human Verdict.** _(Quiet, Confident Valley)_
    _A brutalist architectural anchor, honest invitation, and philosophical colophon._

```

- **Purpose:** Establish the masterbrand authority immediately. Shatter the misconception of flat AI resumes by introducing engineering evaluation as a 3D physical stratification of real code, execution logs, and timing.
- **Visible Composition:**
  - Left 48%: Extreme monumental headline (`clamp(4.2rem, 7.8vw, 8.2rem)`), disciplined kicker (`01 — THE STRATIFIED REALITY`), editorial lead paragraph, and dual primary actions (solid dark ink pill + architectural bordered button).
  - Right 52%: Pinned WebGL viewport displaying three ascending, semi-transparent mineral glass strata tilted at `22°` isometric oblique perspective. Floating between the strata is a clearly illustrative carbon editor buffer, a scripted terminal excerpt derived from an approved fixture, and factual evidence tags floating at distinct depth planes.
  - Background: Daylight paper base (`#F4F1EA`) with faint architectural alignment rules (grid datums) rendered at 0.5px.
- **Typography Behavior:**
  - Headline: Display Serif / Technical Grotesque Hybrid, `font-weight: 500`, tracking `-0.055em`, line-height `0.88`. Text remains pinned while individual letter stems exhibit subtle optical refraction when intersecting the 3D glass strata behind them.
  - Subhead: Functional Monospace / Neutral Sans (`1.05rem`, `line-height: 1.5`, tracking `-0.01em`).
- **Motion & Animation Choreography:**
  - **Entrance (0s - 1.2s):** Page loads with the three STRATA planes collapsed into a single horizontal razor line. Over 1.2s, the planes separate along the Z-axis (`translateZ: 0 -> 240px`) using an ease-out cubic curve (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). The dark IDE workbench slides forward from between plane 1 and plane 2.
  - **Scroll Scrubbing (0% - 100% of scene pin):** As user scrolls, the perspective rotates subtly (`rotateY: -15deg -> -4deg`, `rotateX: 18deg -> 8deg`). The evidence tags (`Attempt`, `Investigation`, `Revision`, `Verification`, `Uncertainty`) drift outward on individual parallax vectors, illustrating that a single commit contains multiple layers of observable reality.
- **Interaction:**
  - Cursor movement creates subtle spatial parallax on the 3D strata (damping: 0.05).
  - Hovering over an evidence tag highlights its corresponding timestamp in the code editor and draws an SVG laser datum connecting the tag to the exact code diff line.
- **Transition In:** Direct browser load with clean SSR hydration; zero white flash; smooth mechanical stratification entrance.
- **Transition Out:** As scroll reaches 100%, the 3D planes compress together into a single ultra-flat, compressed 2D white paper sheet, physically setting up Scene 02.
- **Product Truth Alignment:** Any code or terminal excerpt must come from the supported cache-staleness scenario or be labeled illustrative. No fake "99% match" badges. Tags describe recorded artifacts, not inferred candidate actions or scores.
- **Asset Type:** **E. Combination** (Three.js WebGL canvas for glass refraction and layer depth + DOM overlays for accessible text and buttons).

    1. Plate A: A translucent paper resume where text buzzwords have no underlying evidence foundation.
    2. Plate B: An audio waveform of a 45-minute Q&A interview showing fragmented spoken claims.

  - Hovering on any card expands a small "Missing Context" inspection drawer beneath it using general examples of evidence a proxy artifact cannot establish. It must not present fabricated candidate activity as a recorded session.
- **Transition In:** Clean vertical emergence from Scene 01's flat compression.
- **Transition Out:** The three flat specimen plates fracture along horizontal strata lines and slide laterally offscreen, clearing the stage for the authentic evidence stream in Scene 03.
- **Product Truth Alignment:** Directly attacks proxy metrics, resumes, and automated test checkmarks without insulting the intelligence of engineering leaders.

### Scene 03: OBSERVABLE WORK — The Stratigraphy of an Attempt (Building Rhythm)

```
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  03 — OBSERVABLE REALITY                                                           |
|                                                                                    |
|  Work is not an answer. Work is a trajectory.                                      |
|                                                                                    |
|  [00:00] ───●───────────●────────────●────────────●────────────●─────────► [42:15] |
|           ATTEMPT    INVESTIGATION   FAILURE    REVISION    VERIFICATION           |
|                                                                                    |
|  ┌──────────────────┐ ┌──────────────────┐ ┌──────────────────┐ ┌────────────────┐ |
|  | ATTEMPT          | | INVESTIGATION    | | REVISION         | | UNCERTAINTY    | |
|  | Explores DB pool | | Reads connection | | Rewrites retry   | | Candidate re-  | |
|  | configuration    | | leak issue #142  | | backoff loop     | | reads test spec| |
|  └──────────────────┘ └──────────────────┘ └──────────────────┘ └────────────────┘ |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

- **Purpose:** Explain, as brand-level abstraction, that observable work contains attempts, investigation, failure, revision, verification, outcomes, and unresolved questions. These are not domain event types unless backed by the typed evidence model.
- **Visible Composition:**
  - Top: A live, ticking chronological timeline bar spanning across the screen (`00:00:00` to `00:42:15`).
  - Center: An evidentiary pipeline displaying the six constitutional event categories:
    1. **Attempt:** The candidate initiates a specific technical pathway.
    2. **Investigation:** The candidate navigates code, searches logs, or inspects dependencies.
    3. **Failure:** A compiler error, failed unit test, or runtime crash occurs.
    4. **Revision:** The candidate alters course based on observed feedback.
    5. **Verification:** The candidate writes or runs tests to confirm behavior.
    6. **Uncertainty:** Pauses, repeated queries, or discarded approaches where ambiguity existed.
- **Typography Behavior:**
  - Timestamps in sharp monospace tabular numerals (`JetBrains Mono` or similar, `0.85rem`).
  - Category titles in bold uppercase functional sans (`0.75rem`, `letter-spacing: 0.12em`).
- **Motion & Animation Choreography:**
  - A scrubbed playback cursor moves along the timeline as the user scrolls. As the cursor passes each event node, an evidentiary pulse expands, and the corresponding detail card illuminates with crisp mechanical timing (spring physics: `stiffness: 300, damping: 28`).
- **Interaction:**
  - Clicking any node scrubs the simulated playback directly to that moment, displaying the exact terminal command and git diff generated during that event.
- **Transition In:** Timeline rule draws from left to right across the screen as scroll enters.
- **Transition Out:** The horizontal timeline rotates 90 degrees into a vertical chronological spine, becoming the navigational axis for Scene 04.
- **Product Truth Alignment:** Absolute zero scorekeeping. No "Efficiency: 82%". Every item is a factual record of what happened and when.
- **Asset Type:** **A. Native HTML/CSS/SVG + Canvas**.

|          ==================================================                        |

```

- **Purpose:** Demonstrate how Hirearchy presents supported recorded commands, bounded terminal output, workspace transitions, AI interactions, capture gaps, submission, and submitted state as a human-reviewable chronological reconstruction.
- **Visible Composition:**
  - An exploded isometric stack of three distinct structural planes (the STRATA embodiment):
    - **Bottom Slab (Substrate):** Dark carbon slate showing raw terminal logs, sandbox stdout/stderr, and low-level container events streaming continuously.
    - **Middle Slab (Chronology):** Warm semi-translucent glass slab grouping raw events into narrative clusters: "Investigated memory leak", "Patched redis lock timeout", "Executed regression suite".
    - **Top Slab (Evaluator View):** Crisp warm paper plate showing the Hirearchy Software evaluator chronology with expandable evidence references. Audio/video sync is unsupported and must not appear.
- **Typography Behavior:**
  - Floating 3D annotations attached to each stratum via thin leader lines (`0.5px` ink rules).
  - High typographic density with clinical clarity.
- **Motion & Animation Choreography:**
  - Pinned scroll sequence (duration: 150vh).
  - Scroll 0% - 30%: The three layers sit tightly packed.
  - Scroll 30% - 70%: The layers elevate vertically on the Z-axis, revealing the interconnecting data conduits linking raw logs to structured milestones.
  - Scroll 70% - 100%: Vertical alignment rays shoot upward through all three layers, visually proving that every summary item in Hirearchy is directly anchored to raw runtime proof.
- **Interaction:**
  - User can toggle between "Exploded View" and "Coalesced View" with an interactive tactile toggle switch.
- **Transition In:** Vertical spine from Scene 03 anchors the center of the 3D stack.
- **Transition Out:** The top layer smoothly expands to fill the entire viewport, seamlessly becoming the live Hirearchy Software workbench of Scene 05.
- **Product Truth Alignment:** Visualizes the internal evidence chain without claiming that every low-level runtime event is captured or that derived summaries replace authoritative chronology and submitted state.
- **Asset Type:** **B / E. WebGL / CSS 3D Hybrid**.

  - Clickable timeline events select an approved fixture moment and update the displayed diff and recorded terminal excerpt.
  - Expandable evidence cards: Clicking "Terminal Output" expands the raw container stdout stream.
  - No fake AI scores or "Strong Fit" badges anywhere in the interface.
- **Transition In:** Dramatic scale-up from the top stratum of Scene 04.
- **Transition Out:** The workbench gently recedes in perspective, angling backward as three distinct viewport lenses slide into the foreground for Scene 06.
- **Product Truth Alignment:** The only current public scenario source is `scenario-001-cache-staleness`. Any presentation uses an approved frozen fixture or remains explicitly illustrative; it does not claim a race-condition or cache-deadlock product scenario.

## 6. STRATA Usage Rules & Architectural Grammar

The STRATA emblem consists of **three ascending, parallel stepped layers**. This is not a static decorative badge; it is the **fundamental spatial and epistemological grammar** of the entire site.

### 6.1 The Three Constitutional Strata

1. **Stratum 1 (Base / Runtime Substrate):**
   Represents supported source evidence: recorded commands, bounded standard I/O, process exit codes, workspace transitions, AI interaction records, and the submitted diff. Dark carbon tones (`#141416`), dense monospace typography, tabular layouts.
2. **Stratum 2 (Chronological Axis / Sequence):**
   Represents authoritative chronology order. It may show recorded events and conservative typed facts, but not hidden intent, undocumented browsing, pauses, or inferred investigative pathways. Warm mineral glass, connecting datums, SVG alignment conduits.
3. **Stratum 3 (Evaluator Aperture / Legibility):**
   Represents human understanding. The structured, navigable reconstruction where an engineering leader inspects evidence to reach an uncoerced human decision. Warm paper canvas (`#F4F1EA`), refined serif typography, high-contrast ink boundaries.

### 6.2 Structural Invariants

- **Rule of Separation:** When introducing complexity, elements must separate into depth strata rather than piling up horizontally or vertically in flat cards.
- **Rule of Alignment:** When an interaction completes (e.g., code execution passes tests, an evaluator selects a timestamp), the three strata snap into crisp planar alignment.
- **Rule of Provenance:** Any higher-level summary on Stratum 3 must possess a visible structural ray or leader line connecting it directly down to its physical origin on Stratum 1.
- **Prohibition on Repetition:** Do NOT plaster the three-bar logo across every card or button. Use the _spatial behavior_ (three ascending levels, 22-degree oblique depth, aperture corners) rather than repeating the logo mark itself.

  hidden, offscreen, reduced-motion, reduced-data, or after a static resolve.

### WebGL architecture

- **Narrative contribution:** Scene 01 may use refraction and Z separation to
  make the strata metaphor tangible. Scene 07 may use material light only if CSS
  and SVG cannot achieve the approved art direction. Scene 04 does not require
  WebGL. Scene 08 uses a static asset.

  semantic Server Component. It receives immutable visual inputs and owns no
  product data, copy, navigation, or evidence interpretation.
- **Loading:** Render the strongest static DOM/SVG composition first. Load the
  WebGL module only near its scene and after initial content. Never block LCP on
  Three.js, shaders, or textures.
- **Cleanup:** Cancel animation frames; disconnect observers; remove listeners;
  dispose geometries, materials, textures, render targets, and renderer context
  on unmount or fallback.
- **Fallbacks:** Mobile, reduced motion, reduced data, low device memory, WebGL
  failure, and shader failure receive the resolved static composition. Canvas is
  `aria-hidden`; equivalent meaning remains in DOM.
- **Risk:** Transmission shaders, large textures, multiple contexts, and pinned
  render loops threaten LCP, INP, memory, thermal load, and battery. Prototype
  one hero canvas before approving any second WebGL scene.

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA as the release gate. AAA contrast may be pursued for body

### Responsive / reduced-motion architecture

- Desktop WebGL is progressive enhancement. Mobile receives an intentionally
  composed vertical DOM/SVG scene, not a miniature desktop canvas.
- Scene 05 becomes one pane at a time with native controls and the same DOM
  reading order. No horizontal gesture is the only means of navigation.
- Reduced motion shows each scene's strongest resolved composition immediately:
  separated hero strata, complete chronology, assembled workbench, selected
  perspective, and locked campaign statement.
- Disable pins, parallax, cursor tracking, autoplay, smooth scrubbing, animated
  light, and render loops under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- Treat `prefers-reduced-data`, small viewports, coarse pointers, low device
  memory, and WebGL failure as signals to keep the static composition.

### Performance guardrails

- Keep the initial route's authored client JavaScript at or below **100 KiB
  gzip**, excluding framework runtime measured separately. Treat this as a
  budget to verify, not a current measurement.
- Keep Three.js and shader code out of the initial bundle. Lazy-load one hero
  island after semantic content renders; no more than one active WebGL context.
- Hero static fallback is the LCP candidate. Do not preload WebGL code. Preload
  only the single font or hero still proven necessary by measurement.
- Initial hero textures: at most two, each at or below **1024 px** on its longest
  edge and **250 KiB compressed**. No unbounded image sequences; prototype cap is
  **24 frames / 2 MiB total** if a sequence proves necessary.
- Do not run large video and WebGL simultaneously. Any later video stays below
  **4 MiB** for the initial source and loads on interaction or near viewport.
- Pause offscreen animation and cap device pixel ratio at **1.5** for WebGL.
  Measure before increasing geometry or draw-call limits; the earlier 60-draw
  call and 25,000-vertex figures are ceilings, not performance proof.
- Verify LCP, INP, CLS, memory, long tasks, and thermal behavior on representative
  mobile and desktop hardware. Do not promise 60 fps or zero layout shift before
  measurement.
- Keep the marketing route isolated from Candidate and Evaluator data loading,
  authorization, and client bundles.
