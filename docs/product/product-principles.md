# Product principles

1. **Evidence before judgment.** Prefer observable facts and their sources over inferred traits.
2. **Task outcome is not evaluator decision.** Acceptance criteria, tests, task outcome, evaluator decision, and candidate competence are separate concepts.
3. **AI usage is neutral.** Heavy, light, or absent AI use has no automatic positive or negative meaning.
4. **Do not infer invisible mental state.** Describe observable actions; do not claim trust, understanding, copying, or judgment without a defined valid evidence basis.
5. **Chronology is part of the evidence.** Order and timing make individual events meaningful.
6. **The scenario creates the signal.** Product quality depends primarily on realistic situations that elicit meaningful engineering behavior.
7. **The human evaluator owns the decision.** The system presents evidence; only an evaluator records a verdict.
8. **Difficulty belongs in the engineering problem, not the wording.** A candidate should understand the assignment in roughly 60–90 seconds.
9. **Strong candidates must be allowed to surprise the scenario author.** Do not require one workflow or privileged solution.
10. **Prefer factual evidence over interpretive labels.** Generated prose may connect grounded events but must not convert them into unvalidated traits.
